The House

A comfortable base for time together in the glen.

A stunning converted barn with a vast open-plan living area and a roaring open fire, Muir of Pearsie Lodge is made for time together.

The Lodge is available for corporate retreats, private gatherings and family stays, and our team can tailor every detail, from private dining to curated days exploring Angus, whisky and gin tastings and wellness days. Tell us what you would like to experience and we will organise it for you.

Private dining, curated days in Angus, whisky & gin tastings and wellness days can all be arranged.
