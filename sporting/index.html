Sporting · Season October – January

Sporting days in the Angus hills.

Our responsible gamekeeping programme offers well-run shooting days that honour rural tradition while protecting wildlife and supporting long-term conservation.

Every day at Pearsie is run with care for the birds, the dogs, the land and the guns. Our keepering team knows the ground intimately, and days are paced to be enjoyed, from the first drive to the last light.

Sporting day 01

Driven pheasant & partridge

Traditional driven days across varied ground, from woodland drives to high birds off the hill. Days are run for a single team of guns with a friendly, experienced keepering team.

Teams of 8–9 guns · Elevenses & shoot lunch · Beaters, pickers-up & loaders arranged

Sporting day 02

Walked-up & smaller days

Relaxed walked-up days for smaller parties, walking the margins and rough ground with dogs. It is an unhurried way to see the estate.

Parties of 4–6 guns · Ideal for mixed-experience groups · Flexible timings

The season

October – January

Days can be let to established teams or arranged for guests staying on the estate. Tell us your preferred dates and the size of your team and we’ll shape the day around you.

  • Stay, shoot and dine.

    Combine a shooting day with a stay at Muir of Pearsie Lodge, with private dining, a warm fire and drying room for the whole team.

  • Conservation at the heart of it.

    Gamekeeping at Pearsie goes hand in hand with habitat restoration, predator control, tree planting and careful management of the hill, benefiting songbirds, waders and wildlife across the glen.
