About Pearsie

An estate rooted in the glen.

Pearsie Estate is an attractive Scottish estate with deep rural roots, set in the rolling Angus hills of Glen Prosen.

We are committed to sustainable land management, local employment and a future-focused approach that keeps our landscape thriving, from active tree planting and habitat restoration to renewable energy investment, including our developing hydro project.

Across farming, forestry and estate operations, Pearsie champions ethical practice and community connection. Blending heritage with innovation, we offer a peaceful, authentic Scottish experience.

A peaceful, authentic Scottish experience, where sustainability, rural tradition and unforgettable surroundings come together.

Land & forestry

Active tree planting and habitat restoration across the hill, woodland and river margins.

Renewable energy

Investment in renewables, including a developing hydro project on the estate burn.

Responsible sport

A gamekeeping programme that honours rural tradition while protecting wildlife.

Community

Local employment and ethical practice across farming, forestry and estate operations.
