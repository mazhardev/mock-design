Retreats & Workshops

Retreats shaped around you.

Tell us what you would like to experience and we will organise it for you. Based at Muir of Pearsie Lodge, our team can tailor every detail of a retreat, workshop or private gathering.

Corporate retreatsLeadership offsitesPrivate gatheringsFamily celebrationsWellness groups

Muir of Pearsie Lodge sleeps sixteen in eight bedrooms, with a vast open-plan living area, a long dining table and a roaring open fire. It is the natural heart of any retreat.

Around it, we can arrange private dining, tastings, wellness sessions and days out across Angus, so your group can focus on time together. A games room is also available at extra cost.

Tell us what you would like to experience, and we will organise it for you.

Guided walks, castles, golf and the best of the surrounding glens.
