Discover the estate

Pearsie Estate is a traditional Scottish estate with deep rural roots, set in the rolling Angus hills of Glen Prosen. Farming, forestry and sporting have shaped this land for generations, and they still do.

Today we welcome guests to three countryside houses, to well-run shooting days, and to quiet spaces for meetings, retreats and gatherings. Whatever brings you here, we will help you make the most of it.
